# Discover (mobile)

Mode: Operate. Platform: adaptive (Expo, iOS + Android). First surface of the app's visual world.

Audience: weeknight home cook, phone in the kitchen. Job: pick something for this meal that fits the time and equipment they have, then cook it. Constraints: native navigation per OS, real recipes from the site's domain, no invented prices or counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cookbook-margin kitchen companion. Discover asks "What's for dinner?" (breakfast or lunch by time of day) and answers with a horizontal trail of pinned recipe cards, quickest on the left, instead of the category default of photo shelves on white.

OWN-WORLD: Warm sand paper (#F7F3EA) with garden greens: sage (#B7D3A0) fills buttons and selections, green (#5E8B4E) carries hearts, the trail, icons and stops, greenDeep (#3A5E30) is the only colored text. Ink (#2B2A24) for all other text. Source Serif 4 for titles, Rubik for UI and body, Gochi Hand for handwritten notes, minutes and greetings (never instructions). Ink line doodles (stirring pot, equipment, hut, summit flag) stand in for Stephanie's drawings. Photos are rounded squares inside soft tinted cards. Cards tilt slightly with a strip of tape. Motion: springy presses (straighten, wiggle, heart pop with leaves), a sliding time highlight, dotted lines that draw in; all off under Reduce Motion.

STORY: The cook reads the greeting, picks a time (15 · 30 · 1 hr · Any) or flips to breakfast, scans the trail from quickest to longest with handwritten signposts where the time steps up, taps a card, and starts cooking. Below: cooking journeys by cuisine, equipment doodles that filter the trail, and longer weekend cooks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Handwritten greeting, then "What's for" in light serif and the meal word in bold serif with a sage scribble under it, a handwritten meal switch, the time picker, "9 recipes, quickest first →", and the first pinned trail card with its photo, title, heart, and the dotted trail with a handwritten minutes stop. Native tab bar with a soft green pill on the active tab.

FORM: Started from the Garden Guide Map roll (seed:e5fa1ae5391db557); the user then pinned a soft card reference, asked to make it their own with more whimsy (hand-drawn pot reference), and chose the sand + garden-green palette. seed: none (pinned) for the current look, "this looks so pretty" reference plus "more whimsy like [stirring pot drawing]" and "use greens".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
